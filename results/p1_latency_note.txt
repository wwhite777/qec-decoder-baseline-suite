P1 QEC decoder — STREAMING SINGLE-SHOT (online) decode latency
====================================================================================================
Each number below is the distribution of INDIVIDUAL per-shot decode calls (one
syndrome frame in, one correction out), timed one at a time with perf_counter.
This is ONLINE latency. It is NOT the same as the batch-amortized us/round reported
in the baseline CSVs (those time a whole vectorized batch and divide by shots*rounds).
Batch throughput amortizes per-call overhead and reuses warm caches; the online tail
(p95/p99) characterizes the risk of missing a per-frame deadline; keeping up with the
syndrome stream also depends on the arrival rate, buffering and parallelism.

(units: u = microseconds per decode call)

READING THE NUMBERS:
  * MWPM surface is a per-DETECTOR-FRAME decode; the frame already spans all 25 rounds
    of the memory experiment, so to compare to a <1 us/round target divide by 25.
  * BP-OSD code-capacity is a single-shot syndrome decode (no time dimension).
  * BP-OSD phenomenological decodes a whole T-round space-time frame at once, so its
    per-round figure is (value / T). Its tail is dominated by the OSD fallback firing
    on hard syndromes -> heavy right tail (p99 >> median), the key online-latency risk.

CAVEATS (be honest):
  * All timings are single-threaded Python-level wall-clock on a shared CPU; absolute
    values carry interpreter + scheduler overhead and describe THIS implementation in
    this environment; an optimized C++/FPGA deployment must be measured under matched
    settings (its absolute times and its tail shape may both differ).
  * pymatching.decode / ldpc.decode still cross the Python<->C++ boundary once per call;
    a native streaming implementation would remove that fixed overhead uniformly.
  * This measures the classical reference decoders only. A neural decoder's online
    latency must be measured the SAME way (one frame at a time, incl. host<->GPU copy),
    NOT as a large-batch GPU throughput number, or the comparison is invalid.
